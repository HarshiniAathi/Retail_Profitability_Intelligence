14. Discount vs Profitability Analysis

- Records with no discount generated the highest total profit of $326,718.59.
- Discount levels from 0% through 20% had positive total profit.
- Discount levels from 30% through 80% had negative total profit.
- The 70% discount level recorded the largest aggregate loss of $40,300.57 across 424 records.
- The 80% discount level recorded the lowest profit margin at -180.01%.
- These findings indicate an association between higher discount levels and weaker profitability in this dataset. They do not establish that discounts alone caused the losses.
- Recommended follow-up: investigate loss-making products, regional differences, and order-level transactions before recommending discount policy changes.
